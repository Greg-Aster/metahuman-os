# MetaHuman OS Ethical Use Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-31
**Version:** 1.1

## Purpose

MetaHuman OS is intended for consent-based personal agents and digital
personality extensions. It must not be used for deception, abuse, or harm.

## Prohibited uses

### Impersonation without consent

Do not use MetaHuman OS to impersonate a living or deceased person without the
express authorization of that person or the lawful owner of the relevant data.
This includes creating a deceptive persona or training on another person's
writing, voice, likeness, or private data without permission.

### Malicious systems

Do not use MetaHuman OS to build or operate systems intended to harass, defraud,
manipulate, exploit, surveil, or harm people. Do not use it to spread deceptive
content or to evade another system's security and consent boundaries.

Do not make Skynet—or any system intended to remove meaningful human control
over consequential actions.

### Violations of law or rights

Do not use MetaHuman OS to violate applicable law, intellectual-property rights,
privacy rights, contractual restrictions, or the security of another person or
system.

## Operating principles

- **Authenticity:** Clearly represent what the agent is and who controls it.
- **Consent:** Use another person's data, voice, likeness, or communications only
  with appropriate permission.
- **Human control:** Keep authorization, pause, cancellation, and shutdown
  controls proportionate to the possible effect.
- **Data minimization:** Give models, providers, and integrations only the data
  necessary for the task.
- **Evidence and review:** Do not present generated output, inferred state, or an
  attempted action as verified fact or completed work.
- **Harm reduction:** Review the likely consequences before enabling autonomous,
  external, financial, destructive, or physical actions.

## Responsibility and enforcement

MetaHuman OS runs on operator-controlled infrastructure. The project does not
provide centralized monitoring or a remote mechanism that can disable an
installation. The installation operator remains responsible for configuration,
supervision, compliance, and resulting actions. Hosting providers, model
providers, communities, and applicable authorities may independently enforce
their own rules and laws.

## Account commitment

By accepting this policy, you affirm that you will:

- not impersonate another person without consent;
- not create a malicious AI system intended to harm others;
- preserve meaningful human control over consequential actions;
- comply with applicable law and the rights of others; and
- take responsibility for the personas, integrations, and autonomy you enable.
